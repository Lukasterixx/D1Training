# Decision gates

From [the Thesis B plan](../docs/thesis_b_plan.md#gates-and-measurement-definitions). Criteria are
proposed engineering targets until frozen: task-specific values by Week 2, contact calibration by
Week 3. A gate passes only on the evidence it names. Interface smoke runs and training-time
diagnostics do not pass G1.

G4–G7 were added by the plan revision of 16 September, which made a physical AprilTag-guided
combiner-box demonstration the Thesis B deliverable. They are planned work; none of the hardware,
camera or perception they describe has been brought up in this repository.
