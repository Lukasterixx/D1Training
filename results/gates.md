# Decision gates

From [the Thesis B plan](../docs/thesis_b_plan.md#gates-and-measurement-definitions). Criteria are
proposed engineering targets until frozen (planned for Week 6). A gate passes only on the evidence
it names. Interface smoke runs and training-time diagnostics do not pass G1.

| Gate | Criterion | Status | Evidence and notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G0 · Environment works | Correct articulation/action mapping; finite state; deliberate timeout/fall and partial-reset tests pass; no clone interference; target-frame checks pass | in progress | 2026-09-15: first simulator runs ([Week 1](week_01/notes.md)). `verify` passes 20/20 (F-009): action order and 66/87 widths, 10 Hz arm hold and feedback, leg delays, each termination induced with only that environment resetting, Link6 kinematics and link masses against the CPU model. The arm drive type was fixed along the way (F-010), and a self-collision positive control shows no resting overlap from the weld. The controlled point is now the Link7_1 pincer tip (CAD), matching the model to 0.9 µm (F-013, verify 21/21). Still open: a visual inspection of posture and target marker (screenshot); clone interference, shown so far only as unaffected control environments |
| G1a · Free-space baseline | 100 frozen 10 s episodes per seed: ≥90% reach within 5 cm for ≥1 s continuous; survive to episode end; ≤1% falls | not started | Needs the frozen-manifest evaluator (not yet written). Zero actions meet the 5 cm criterion for 12.9% of targets with the pincer tip (256 environments, F-013), because every reset slides the robot 7.4 cm back. Move the box and report a zero-action baseline before this gate means anything |
| G1b · Moving baseline | G1a criteria on slow trajectories, plus commanded-versus-measured base velocity | not started | Also report gait quality (foot vs neutral point, front–rear spacing, backward after request, turn tracking at several rates, pitch wobble). In playback, yaw is where the welded arm shows first: 31° peak tilt at 1 rad/s against 16° bare (F-012) |
