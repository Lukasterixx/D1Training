Smooth motion investigation, 2026-09-16. Go2 sitting, arm-only.
Lukas: "not smoothly moving to its targets, but rather stepping which causes wobble."

--- why a per-cycle loop steps
A single funcode 2 mode 0 waypoint is smooth. J0, 30 deg, per-sample speed (deg/s):
    0, 28, 67.5, 69.3, 69.3, 34.3, 1.8, 0        (~0.55 s, one trapezoid)
Two of the 111 ms cycles go on acceleration alone. A waypoint issued every cycle
restarts that profile, so the arm never leaves the accelerating phase and every
cycle is an accel/decel pair -- the stepping, and the wobble.

--- step cap sweep, same move [0.20, 0.18, 0.30], measured on hardware
  cap  travelled/cycle  speed     duty   verdict
   5     2.5 deg        21 deg/s  0.30   STEPPING
   8     4.4 deg        37 deg/s  0.54   STEPPING
  12     4.9 deg        42 deg/s  0.60   STEPPING
  16     5.3 deg        46 deg/s  0.64   STEPPING   <- plateau
Speed-limited displacement would be 7.8-8.3 deg/cycle. Lukas, watching: "the steps
were smaller just then but still there."

--- single shot (one waypoint per pass)
  outbound 45.0 deg excursion -> 4.87 mm, 7.8 deg/cycle at 70 deg/s, duty 1.00 CONTINUOUS
  return   44.6 deg excursion -> 4.18 mm, 7.9 deg/cycle at 71 deg/s, duty 1.00 CONTINUOUS
  second pass skipped both times: the 0.49/0.51 deg correction is below the arm's floor.

--- two bugs found and regression-tested while building it
1. _await_still counted the 60-130 ms command latency as arrival, so it returned
   before the arm began moving (reported 129 mm and 93 mm errors).
2. It then counted REPEATS of a cached feedback sample as stillness: the loop can
   spin faster than the 111 ms feedback, so stale reads look like a stationary arm.
   This made a completed move report 199.73 mm; the arm had in fact arrived.
   Stillness is now judged only on fresh samples (client.poll() returning True).
